Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 9, 2025
Contact: team@mintcashback.com

Mint Cashback ("we", "us", or "our") is a browser extension that helps users earn cash back while shopping online. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect:
- Email address (for account management and rewards)
- Order details from supported stores (to confirm cashback)
- Basic device and browser information (for security and support)

We Do Not Collect:
- Credit card or payment information
- Unrelated browsing history
- Sensitive personal data

How We Use Your Information:
- To track and issue cashback
- To communicate about your account or rewards
- To improve and secure our service

How We Protect Your Information:
- Industry-standard encryption
- Minimal data collection
- We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing

Cookies and Tracking:
- Used only to track eligible purchases and keep you logged in
- Google Analytics
- No third-party ad tracking

Your Rights:
- You may access, update, or delete your information at any time
- You may opt out of marketing emails
- Contact us at team@mintcashback.com for privacy questions

Minors:
- Mint Cashback is for users 18 and older
- We do not knowingly collect data from children

Policy Changes:
- We will notify you of major changes via the extension or email

Questions:
- Email us at team@mintcashback.com
